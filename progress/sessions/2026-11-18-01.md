## Teaching checkpoint 2

At `x=2, y=3, w=1, b=0`, the prediction is `ŷ=2` and the loss is `L=1`.

- First step: because `ŷ=wx+b`, a change in `w` gives the exact prediction change `Δŷ=xΔw=2Δw`.
- Second step: at `ŷ=2`, `∂L/∂ŷ=2(ŷ-y)=-2`, so for a small prediction change, `ΔL≈-2Δŷ`.
- Substitute the first change into the second: `ΔL≈(-2)(2Δw)=-4Δw`.
- Meaning: the first rate converts a weight change into a prediction change; the second rate converts that prediction change into a loss change. The rates multiply because the changes happen one after another along the same path.
- This explanation is tutor-supplied. Learner verification is pending.
